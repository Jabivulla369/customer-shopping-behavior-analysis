## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes customer shopping behavior using transactional data from 3,900 purchases across various product categories[cite: 2]. The primary goal is to uncover actionable insights into spending patterns, customer segments, product preferences, and subscription behavior to guide strategic business decisions[cite: 2].

---

## 📊 Dataset Summary
* **Total Rows:** 3,900[cite: 2].
* **Total Columns:** 18[cite: 2].
* **Key Features:** 
  * **Customer Demographics:** Age, Gender, Location, and Subscription Status[cite: 2].
  * **Purchase Details:** Item Purchased, Category, Purchase Amount, Season, Size, and Color[cite: 2].
  * **Shopping Behavior:** Discount Applied, Promo Code Used, Previous Purchases, Frequency of Purchases, Review Rating, and Shipping Type[cite: 2].
* **Missing Data:** Identified 37 missing values in the `Review Rating` column[cite: 2].

---

## 🐍 Exploratory Data Analysis & Preparation (Python)
The data preparation and cleaning workflow was executed in Python[cite: 2]:
* **Data Loading & Inspection:** Imported the dataset using `pandas`, checking dataset structure with `df.info()` and summary statistics with `df.describe()`[cite: 2].
* **Missing Data Handling:** Imputed missing values in the `Review Rating` column using the median rating of each specific product category to prevent bias[cite: 2].
* **Column Standardization:** Renamed columns to snake case for better readability and cleaner documentation[cite: 2].
* **Feature Engineering:** 
  * Created the `age_group` column by binning customer ages[cite: 2].
  * Created the `purchase_frequency_days` column derived from purchase data[cite: 2].
* **Data Consistency Check:** Verified that `discount_applied` and `promo_code_used` contained redundant information, leading to the removal of `promo_code_used`[cite: 2].
* **Database Integration:** Connected the Python environment to PostgreSQL and loaded the cleaned DataFrame into a database table for SQL-based processing[cite: 2].

## 🔍 Data Analysis & Business Queries (SQL)
Structured analysis was conducted in PostgreSQL to answer 10 essential business questions[cite: 2]:
1. **Revenue by Gender:** Compared total revenue generated by male ($157,890) versus female ($75,191) customers[cite: 2].
2. **High-Spending Discount Users:** Identified 839 customers who used discounts yet still spent above the average purchase amount[cite: 2].
3. **Top 5 Products by Rating:** Highlighted products with the highest average review ratings (Gloves at 3.86, Sandals at 3.84, Boots at 3.82, Hat at 3.80, and Skirt at 3.78)[cite: 2].
4. **Shipping Type Comparison:** Compared average purchase amounts between Standard ($58.46) and Express ($60.48) shipping[cite: 2].
5. **Subscribers vs. Non-Subscribers:** Analyzed customer counts, average spend, and total revenue across subscription statuses (1,053 subscribers yielding $62,645; 2,847 non-subscribers yielding $170,436)[cite: 2].
6. **Discount-Dependent Products:** Determined the top 5 products most frequently sold with discounts (Hat at 50.00%, Sneakers at 49.66%, Coat at 49.07%, Sweater at 48.17%, and Pants at 47.37%)[cite: 2].
7. **Customer Segmentation:** Classified 3,900 customers into segments based on purchase history (3,116 Loyal, 701 Returning, and 83 New customers)[cite: 2].
8. **Top 3 Products per Category:** Utilized window functions (`ROW_NUMBER()`) partitioned by category to extract top-selling products by order count[cite: 2].
9. **Repeat Buyers & Subscriptions:** Evaluated whether repeat buyers (>5 purchases) subscribe, showing 2,518 non-subscribers versus 958 subscribers in this tier[cite: 2].
10. **Revenue by Age Group:** Calculated total revenue contributions across demographic age tiers (Young Adult: $62,143; Middle-aged: $59,197; Adult: $55,978; Senior: $55,763)[cite: 2].

## 📈 Interactive Dashboard (Power BI)
An interactive dashboard was built in Power BI to visually present core insights[cite: 2]:
* **KPI Summary Cards:** Displayed total number of customers (3.9K), average purchase amount ($59.76), and average review rating (3.75)[cite: 2].
* **Subscription Status:** Donut chart breaking down customers into Yes (27%) and No (73%) subscription tiers[cite: 2].
* **Category & Age Visuals:** Clustered column and bar charts illustrating revenue and sales distribution across product categories and age groups[cite: 2].
* **Interactive Slicers:** Multi-select filters for Subscription Status, Gender, Category, and Shipping Type to dynamically filter all dashboard metrics[cite: 2].

## 💡 Business Recommendations
Based on the end-to-end analysis, the following strategic actions are recommended[cite: 2]:
* **Boost Subscriptions:** Promote exclusive membership benefits to capture the large segment of non-subscribing repeat buyers[cite: 2].
* **Customer Loyalty Programs:** Design reward structures to cultivate and transition customers from "Returning" to the "Loyal" segment[cite: 2].
* **Review Discount Policy:** Re-evaluate high-discount items (like Hats and Sneakers) to balance sales volume with profit margin control[cite: 2].
* **Product Positioning:** Highlight top-rated products (such as Gloves and Sandals) prominently in marketing campaigns[cite: 2].
* **Targeted Marketing:** Focus acquisition and retention campaigns on high-revenue demographics (Young Adults) and high-spend delivery users (Express shipping)[cite: 2].
