## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes customer shopping behavior using transactional data from 3,900 purchases across various product categories. The primary goal is to uncover actionable insights into spending patterns, customer segments, product preferences, and subscription behavior to guide strategic business decisions.

---

## 📊 Dataset Summary
* **Total Rows:** 3,900.
* **Total Columns:** 18.
* **Key Features:** 
  * **Customer Demographics:** Age, Gender, Location, and Subscription Status.
  * **Purchase Details:** Item Purchased, Category, Purchase Amount, Season, Size, and Color.
  * **Shopping Behavior:** Discount Applied, Promo Code Used, Previous Purchases, Frequency of Purchases, Review Rating, and Shipping Type.
* **Missing Data:** Identified 37 missing values in the `Review Rating` column.

---

## 🐍 Exploratory Data Analysis & Preparation (Python)
The data preparation and cleaning workflow was executed in Python:
* **Data Loading & Inspection:** Imported the dataset using `pandas`, checking dataset structure with `df.info()` and summary statistics with `df.describe()`.
* **Missing Data Handling:** Imputed missing values in the `Review Rating` column using the median rating of each specific product category to prevent bias.
* **Column Standardization:** Renamed columns to snake case for better readability and cleaner documentation.
* **Feature Engineering:** 
  * Created the `age_group` column by binning customer ages.
  * Created the `purchase_frequency_days` column derived from purchase data.
* **Data Consistency Check:** Verified that `discount_applied` and `promo_code_used` contained redundant information, leading to the removal of `promo_code_used`.
* **Database Integration:** Connected the Python environment to PostgreSQL and loaded the cleaned DataFrame into a database table for SQL-based processing.

## 🔍 Data Analysis & Business Queries (SQL)
Structured analysis was conducted in PostgreSQL to answer 10 essential business questions:
1. **Revenue by Gender:** Compared total revenue generated by male ($157,890) versus female ($75,191) customers.
2. **High-Spending Discount Users:** Identified 839 customers who used discounts yet still spent above the average purchase amount.
3. **Top 5 Products by Rating:** Highlighted products with the highest average review ratings (Gloves at 3.86, Sandals at 3.84, Boots at 3.82, Hat at 3.80, and Skirt at 3.78).
4. **Shipping Type Comparison:** Compared average purchase amounts between Standard ($58.46) and Express ($60.48) shipping.
5. **Subscribers vs. Non-Subscribers:** Analyzed customer counts, average spend, and total revenue across subscription statuses (1,053 subscribers yielding $62,645; 2,847 non-subscribers yielding $170,436).
6. **Discount-Dependent Products:** Determined the top 5 products most frequently sold with discounts (Hat at 50.00%, Sneakers at 49.66%, Coat at 49.07%, Sweater at 48.17%, and Pants at 47.37%).
7. **Customer Segmentation:** Classified 3,900 customers into segments based on purchase history (3,116 Loyal, 701 Returning, and 83 New customers).
8. **Top 3 Products per Category:** Utilized window functions (`ROW_NUMBER()`) partitioned by category to extract top-selling products by order count.
9. **Repeat Buyers & Subscriptions:** Evaluated whether repeat buyers (>5 purchases) subscribe, showing 2,518 non-subscribers versus 958 subscribers in this tier.
10. **Revenue by Age Group:** Calculated total revenue contributions across demographic age tiers (Young Adult: $62,143; Middle-aged: $59,197; Adult: $55,978; Senior: $55,763).

## 📈 Interactive Dashboard (Power BI)
An interactive dashboard was built in Power BI to visually present core insights:
* **KPI Summary Cards:** Displayed total number of customers (3.9K), average purchase amount ($59.76), and average review rating (3.75).
* **Subscription Status:** Donut chart breaking down customers into Yes (27%) and No (73%) subscription tiers.
* **Category & Age Visuals:** Clustered column and bar charts illustrating revenue and sales distribution across product categories and age groups.
* **Interactive Slicers:** Multi-select filters for Subscription Status, Gender, Category, and Shipping Type to dynamically filter all dashboard metrics.

## 💡 Business Recommendations
Based on the end-to-end analysis, the following strategic actions are recommended:
* **Boost Subscriptions:** Promote exclusive membership benefits to capture the large segment of non-subscribing repeat buyers.
* **Customer Loyalty Programs:** Design reward structures to cultivate and transition customers from "Returning" to the "Loyal" segment.
* **Review Discount Policy:** Re-evaluate high-discount items (like Hats and Sneakers) to balance sales volume with profit margin control.
* **Product Positioning:** Highlight top-rated products (such as Gloves and Sandals) prominently in marketing campaigns.
* **Targeted Marketing:** Focus acquisition and retention campaigns on high-revenue demographics (Young Adults) and high-spend delivery users (Express shipping).
